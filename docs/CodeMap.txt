- Publishes one Ready CodeServiceClientSession only after all validation succeeds and arms exactly one event-driven Process.Exited observation on its retained exact Process instance. Arm and callback activation are separate phases: the handler plus EnableRaisingEvents are established while Ready identity is serialized under the coordinator lock, but callback admission is activated only after that lock and Ready publication callback boundary are clear. No long-lived watcher Task, watcher CancellationTokenSource, _Process work, timer, polling, repeated PID lookup, or HTTP health ping exists.
